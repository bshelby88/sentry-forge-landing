3–7 yrs

Questions

Sharp answers. No dodges.

Will this hurt my credit more?
No. Disputing a tradeline cannot lower your score. It can only stay the same, get marked “disputed,” or get deleted.
Is the debt really gone if you delete the tradeline?
The tradeline coming off your report is one outcome. The underlying debt may still legally exist (until SOL or settlement). Sentry Forge attacks both fronts: tradeline deletion AND enforceability defenses.
What if I actually owe the money?
We tell you. We won’t help you fight a clean debt with no defects. We will help you negotiate it down with documented leverage — settlement at 20-40¢ on the dollar is common when the SOL is close or itemization is weak.
What states do you cover?
All 50, with state-specific SOL, eviction, and consumer-protection layered in. Louisiana, Texas, Florida, California, and New York have the most case law loaded today.
What if the collector ignores me?
Their silence is the violation. That’s why the FDCPA exists. We escalate to CFPB, state AG, and the credit bureaus in parallel — silence kills their tradeline.
What if I get sued?
First, run the SOL check — most “lawsuit threats” are bluff on time-barred debt. Second, your full pack hands an attorney a 60% finished defense. Third, we maintain a referral list of consumer-defense attorneys who take FDCPA cases on contingency.
Is this legal advice?
No. Sentry Forge is consumer education and document drafting. We do not establish an attorney-client relationship. For litigation, get a licensed attorney in your state.
